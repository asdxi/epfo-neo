# EPFO Neo Product Principles

These principles were restated by Abhishek on 6 September 2026. Apply them before making any product, interaction, information-architecture or workflow change.

Before acting, ask:

1. Is this aligned with Abhishek's product vision, including his frustrations with the current EPFO system and the changes he intends to see?
2. Does this make the product simpler to use?
3. Does this add meaningful value?
4. Does this move beyond the current EPFO portal rather than repeat the same workflow in a newer wrapper?
5. Is the change both meaningfully different and legally and operationally feasible?

Proceed only when every answer is supported with greater than 90% confidence. Otherwise, pause and discuss the uncertainty before implementation.

## Working Interpretation

- EPFO Neo is the end-to-end EPFO member portal, not a third-party layer speaking about EPFO or Neo.
- The product should absorb system complexity and present clear facts, actions and resolution paths.
- Home should remain light: show the member's current situation and the paths that matter now. Put detailed explanations on the relevant dedicated page.
- Prefer fewer words without losing meaning. Use familiar English and avoid internal or technical language.
- Deterministic financial and workflow records remain the source of truth. Never invent an amount, cause, obligation, acknowledgement, transfer result or EPS consequence.
- Improve on the current portal only where the change is useful, feasible and trustworthy—not merely visually different.
